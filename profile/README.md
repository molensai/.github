## Create videos from an idea or an image

Video creation is the main focus of Molens AI. Choose the starting point that fits your project, describe what should happen in the shot, and continue into Studio to configure your generation.

### Text to video

Start with a description when you want to explore a scene before you have a reference image. Explain the subject, environment, camera movement, lighting, and action in one focused prompt.

For example, describe a quiet reading corner at dusk, a fixed camera, and a curtain moving gently beside the window. Keeping one clear action makes the result easier to review and revise.

### Image to video

Start with an image when the subject or composition already exists. Upload the visual and describe the movement you want to add, such as a restrained camera approach or a subtle change in the scene.

This workflow is useful for photo animation, visual teasers, and motion assets based on an existing creative direction. Review the result against the reference, especially where faces, product shapes, or text need to remain accurate.

### Product video

Develop short video concepts around a product and the message you want viewers to notice. Use a clear product reference, a specific audience, and a verified feature or benefit to shape the brief.

The product video workflow also helps you prepare and edit three template-based creative directions before moving into generation. Use those directions to explore a product overview, a detail, or a surrounding scene. A generated concept is a candidate to review, not a prediction of advertising results.

## A practical video workflow

1. **Choose your starting point.** Use text for a new scene or a reference image for an existing visual.
2. **Describe one shot.** State the subject, movement, framing, and details that matter.
3. **Review the settings.** Check the selected model, available options, and displayed credit cost before generating.
4. **Inspect the output.** Watch the complete video and check subject consistency, motion, composition, and any visible text.
5. **Prepare the final asset.** Make any needed edits and review the finished crop before using it in a post, page, or campaign.

Available settings and credit requirements depend on the selected generation options. See the website for [current plans](https://molensai.com/pricing).
